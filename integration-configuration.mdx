The **Partner** is the application or service integrating with Tavi. The **Customer** is the person or organization requesting work. The **Approver** authorizes spending. The **Payer** settles the invoice. These roles may belong to different parties.

## Configure your integration

| Area | Agree during onboarding |
| --- | --- |
| Customer identity | Stable customer and organization IDs; isolate access across organizations |
| Service coverage | Share the locations and trades you need; confirm coverage with Tavi during setup |
| Sites and units | Stable site IDs, optional unit IDs and customer contact details: phone required, email optional |
| Pricing and approvals | Rate-card services, inclusions, diagnostic fees, quote approval, spending authority and optional PO references |
| Billing | Billed entity, payer, payment method, fees, tax and settlement terms |
| Customer communication | Tavi contacts the customer directly by phone or message; agree service hours and human escalation |
| Concierge activity | Use webhooks to keep photos, approvals and work-order updates in your application |
| Intake | Request description, location, customer phone number, trade and target date; Tavi clarifies the issue with the customer |
| Completion | Work report, photos, customer review and follow-up |
| Technical setup | Sandbox, scoped credentials, callback URL, signing, retries, limits and retention |

## Locations and contacts

Use a stable site reference. Where a site contains multiple units, keep unit identity separate from the occupant or contact. Group units under their building address. Include the customer’s phone number on every request; email is optional.

## Test before launch

Run representative jobs through intake, scheduling, approval, evidence, customer review and invoicing. Exercise duplicate requests, failed callbacks, cancellation, not-fixed reports and payment failures. Confirm service hours and support ownership before launch.
