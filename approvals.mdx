## Rate card or quote

| Pricing path | Tavi provides | Approval needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rate-card service | A matching service at the agreed price, with its inclusions and exclusions | Service scope and price |
| Quoted work | A defined scope and quote for work outside the rate card | Scope and total price |
| Diagnosis needed | A diagnostic visit and fee, followed by a repair quote | Diagnostic fee, then repair scope and price |
| Scope or cost changes | An explanation and updated total before additional work | Changed scope and revised total |

We aim to agree a shared rate card for routine services. The customer describes the problem; Tavi identifies the appropriate service. Your backend records the approved service or quote, its version and the decision. Agree who can authorize spending during onboarding.

## Approve spending before work starts

A not-to-exceed (NTE) limit authorizes a defined scope of work up to a maximum total. Get approval for changes to the scope or spending limit before carrying out the additional work. Use `quote_required` to request a quote before authorizing work. Include diagnostic fees in the authorization. Paid work starts after the designated approver accepts the scope and price. This also applies to emergency work.

A quote decision identifies the **quote version, selected option, actor, time, evidence and new total authorized amount**. Reject stale or expired quotes. The new authorized total includes costs already incurred. Record the scope, amount and currency as structured fields.

Record the customer’s review of the completed work. If the issue remains, Tavi reviews the report and arranges follow-up.
